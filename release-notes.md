## Miller Driver Updater 2.2.0

- **Updates itself.** When a newer version is published, a one-line banner offers it. Update downloads it from this
  page, checks it against GitHub's SHA-256 checksum, installs it quietly and reopens the app. If your antivirus removes
  the new version, the current one is kept and reopened.
- Renamed from *Miller Driver Update* (2.1). Installing over an older version keeps its reports, driver backups and
  weekly check.

Install: download `MillerDriverUpdater-2.2.0-Setup.exe` below and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC":
click *More info*, then *Run anyway* (the app is not code-signed yet).
